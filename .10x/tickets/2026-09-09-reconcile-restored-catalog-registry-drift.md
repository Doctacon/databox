# Reconcile restored-catalog registry drift

## Problem

The first live registry-derived restored-catalog validation loaded and read all 22 canonical expectations successfully, but correctly failed because restored Polaris enumerated seven additional tables:

- generated eBird child tables: `raw_ebird.taxonomy__banding_codes`, `raw_ebird.taxonomy__com_name_codes`, and `raw_ebird.taxonomy__sci_name_codes`;
- excluded USFWS tables: `raw_usfws._dlt_load_status`, `raw_usfws.image_records`, and `raw_usfws.image_search_runs`;
- probe table: `dlt_polaris_probe.events`.

The eBird child tables are generated by the declared taxonomy resource and may expose an incomplete canonical `raw_tables` contract. They are not equivalent to the other four tables. The canonical registry specification explicitly excludes USFWS from orchestrated Databox registry ownership, and the probe namespace is validation state rather than a canonical source.

## Scope

1. Declare exactly these deterministic dlt-generated eBird taxonomy child tables in the canonical source registry: `taxonomy__banding_codes`, `taxonomy__com_name_codes`, and `taxonomy__sci_name_codes`.
2. Update registry-derived generated artifacts and tests required by that declaration.
3. Do not change the treatment of USFWS or probe state; that separate policy is owned by `.10x/tickets/2026-09-09-classify-noncanonical-restored-catalog-state.md`.
4. Do not mutate Polaris, Iceberg objects, or recovery artifacts.
5. After implementation and independent review, require separate authorization for any new live restored-catalog validation.

## Acceptance criteria

- The eBird registry entry declares the three observed deterministic taxonomy child tables in addition to `taxonomy`.
- Registry-derived validation expects those tables without adding a second table list.
- Registry and generated-artifact tests pass.
- USFWS and probe tables remain outside the canonical registry and no validator warning/failure policy changes.
