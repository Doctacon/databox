# Reconcile restored-catalog registry drift

## Problem

The first live registry-derived restored-catalog validation loaded and read all 22 canonical expectations successfully, but correctly failed because restored Polaris enumerated seven additional tables:

- generated eBird child tables: `raw_ebird.taxonomy__banding_codes`, `raw_ebird.taxonomy__com_name_codes`, and `raw_ebird.taxonomy__sci_name_codes`;
- excluded USFWS tables: `raw_usfws._dlt_load_status`, `raw_usfws.image_records`, and `raw_usfws.image_search_runs`;
- probe table: `dlt_polaris_probe.events`.

The eBird child tables are generated by the declared taxonomy resource and may expose an incomplete canonical `raw_tables` contract. They are not equivalent to the other four tables. The canonical registry specification explicitly excludes USFWS from orchestrated Databox registry ownership, and the probe namespace is validation state rather than a canonical source.

## Scope

1. Determine whether all deterministic dlt-generated child tables must be declared by the canonical source registry, and update the executable contract/tests only after approval.
2. Keep USFWS and probe state explicitly separate from registry-owned source expectations; decide through review whether non-owned namespaces must be retired from Polaris, quarantined, or classified without weakening fail-closed recovery validation.
3. Add tests that preserve the distinction between generated child-table contract drift and genuinely excluded/noncanonical catalog state.
4. Do not mutate Polaris, Iceberg objects, the canonical registry, or recovery artifacts until the policy and any cleanup action receive separate approval.
5. After repair and independent review, require separate authorization for a new live restored-catalog validation.

## Acceptance criteria

- The canonical source contract accounts for every deterministic registry-owned table without adding USFWS or probe state as orchestrated sources.
- Unexpected noncanonical namespaces remain visible and fail closed unless an approved policy explicitly defines otherwise.
- Hermetic tests distinguish eBird generated children from excluded USFWS/probe tables.
- A separately authorized live rerun has no missing, unexpected, malformed, or unreadable registry-owned state before any cutover claim.

## Exclusions

- Catalog/table deletion or mutation.
- Source refresh or warehouse write.
- Adding USFWS to the seven-source Databox registry.
- Production cutover, artifact cleanup, or timed RPO/RTO claims.
